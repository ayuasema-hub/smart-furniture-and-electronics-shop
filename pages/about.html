About Furniture Roomstore

Furniture Roomstore is an online catalogue for browsing furniture and home electronics. This website presents product information and a frontend shopping demonstration.

Product availability, specifications, delivery arrangements, returns, and payment processing require confirmation through a connected store service. No showroom or company history is represented here.

The catalogue includes furniture and electronics categories in Ethiopian birr (ETB), with product pages and a local cart that can support future backend integration.
