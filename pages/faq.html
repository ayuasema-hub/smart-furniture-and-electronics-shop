Frequently asked questions

Are payments processed on this website?

No. Checkout is a frontend demonstration and does not connect to Telebirr, CBE Birr, or a payment processor.

Does checkout create a persistent order?

No. The demo creates a temporary reference in this browser session. It is not stored in a store order system.

How can I confirm delivery or returns?

Verified store contact and policy information has not been supplied. Confirm arrangements with the store before purchase.
