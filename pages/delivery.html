How delivery is arranged

  1. Choose products and note their names or page links, quantities, and your delivery city.
  2. Ask about delivery through a customer contact channel confirmed by the business. This website cannot submit a delivery request.
  3. The business should confirm product availability, whether delivery can be arranged for your address, any charge, and a proposed schedule before you agree.
  4. Confirm the final arrangement directly with the business. Payment is handled outside this website.
